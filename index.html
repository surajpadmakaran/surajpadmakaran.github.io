Why this blog exists

the short version

I was on the other side of this once. I was not a security person; I did not know how these attacks worked, and I could have been fooled by the same calls and messages that fool people every day. Now I work in cybersecurity — and this blog is how I bring everyone else across with me.

Most security writing is written for security teams. This one is written for everyone else: for the senior deciding whether that call is really her bank (it almost never is), for the youngster opening a first account, for the family that shares one Wi-Fi network and a dozen passwords. If my memoir sent you here — welcome. Start with these:

About the author

the short version

Suraj Padmakaran

Cybersecurity · GRC · Security Automation

Suraj Padmakaran is a cybersecurity professional specializing in governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) and security automation. He builds the pipelines, scripts, and workflows that replace manual compliance toil — turning scan data, evidence collection, and remediation tracking into systems that run themselves. His public writing has a second, larger aim: cybersecurity education for everyone — above all the seniors, youngsters and families that attackers target most.

His work sits at the intersection of audit rigor and engineering craft: controls that stay continuously evidenced, findings that route themselves to owners, and reports that assemble before anyone asks. Teams regularly bring him the compliance workflows nobody else wants to untangle — and leave with automations that give them their week back.

He writes here about what actually works in the field: the unglamorous scripts, the design decisions behind them, and the lessons learned shipping automation into real security programs.
